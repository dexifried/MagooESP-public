Your crawler, on your phone.

Control Magoo and view your dashboard from your Android phone. The app includes phone GPS sharing.

Download for Android
Android 8 or newer · Install from this site when prompted.

Get connected

  1. Download and open the APK. Android may ask you to allow installs from your browser.
  2. Connect your phone and Magoo to the same Wi-Fi, or connect your phone to Magoo’s Wi-Fi.
  3. Open Magoo. Use the Magoo address setting if your phone cannot find it automatically.
