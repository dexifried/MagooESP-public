Connect your phone to Magoo

A direct connection lets you change Magoo’s Wi-Fi settings.

1. Open your phone’s Wi-Fi settings.

2. Join MagooESP using your Magoo setup password.

3. Return here and tap the button below.

Your phone may say this network has no internet. Stay connected to finish setup.
